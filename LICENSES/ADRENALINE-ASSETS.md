# Adrenaline visual assets

`paper-grain.webp`, `dark-organic.webp`, and `warning-stripe.webp` were
generated as original texture tiles with OpenAI's built-in image generation
tool on 2026-09-10 for this repository. They contain no scan, logo, text, or
fragment from _Z1L04 Livret Police.pdf_.

Prompts, abridged only for layout:

- Paper: seamless warm ivory fibrous paper, quiet behind long text; no stains,
  text, symbols, logos, watermark, or source-page reproduction.
- Dark: seamless near-black charcoal with faint oxblood organic veins; no
  anatomy, gore, text, logos, watermark, or source-page reproduction.
- Warning: seamless muted ochre/charcoal diagonal screen-print bands; no words,
  logos, symbols, watermark, or photoreal tape.

The generated PNG sources were resized and encoded as WebP. These three WebP
files are distributed under the repository's MIT license.
